# Feature workflow

Follow the approved Superpowers design and implementation plan. Register `type: spec`, a unique `spec-...` ID, title, current native session ID, and `complexity: simple` or `complex`.

A simple workflow remains in plan until the implementation plan is approved, then follows plan → implement → verify → learn → complete. A complex workflow uses plan → discovery → design → governance → plan, then follows implement → verify → learn → complete after approval. Store approved plan/design content through the available tools. Keep ordered tasks and explicit completion updates in Stratus. On failed review return verify → implement and fix the findings. Do not change the server's phase graph or treat hook success as approval. If the team has design review enabled, the phase transition out of design (complex, to governance) and the transition from plan to implement or accept (simple, and complex for the design documents) returns 409 `design_review_required` until the reviewer chosen for the team passes the current documents; Stratus starts the review and sends FAIL feedback to the author automatically. Do not work around it: wait for the team results and retry the transition. After a FAIL rework of the plan, re-present the revised plan to the user before transitioning. If the 409 says the author is unknown, revise the document yourself and retry. If it says the review is stuck or exhausted, or reports a document problem, ask the user: they can override or transition from the dashboard.
